# Prompt — Starbucks × Mount Fuji Watercolor Print

Reference image: `94914ce8-11c9-4766-8c45-654ac25aaf2d.png`

## Brief

Use the uploaded image as the exact composition and subject reference.
Transform the original nighttime photograph into a delicate hand-painted
watercolor art print, while preserving the recognizable scene and all major
elements.

Recreate the Starbucks café centered in the foreground, with its dark
low-profile building, clearly visible STARBUCKS sign, glowing warm interior
lights, outdoor seating/windows, surrounding evergreen trees, minimalist
street lamps, and the majestic snow-covered Mount Fuji rising directly behind
the café. Preserve the original perspective, proportions, placement, and
atmospheric depth.

## Style

Traditional Japanese-inspired watercolor illustration on warm ivory textured
paper, elegant and minimal, with soft translucent washes, subtle pigment
bleeding, visible paper grain, loose hand-painted brushwork, and naturally
imperfect watercolor edges.

## Palette

A restrained winter palette of deep cobalt blue, icy blue, soft cyan, muted
navy, pale gray, and warm golden yellow from the café windows and signage.

- Paint Mount Fuji with delicate blue-gray snow shadows and atmospheric mist
  around its lower slopes.
- Keep the sky beautifully simplified into smooth watercolor gradients rather
  than photographic detail.

## Composition

Premium minimalist stationery / gallery art-print composition with the
illustration relatively small and centered, surrounded by generous blank
cream-colored paper.

## Typography

Under the illustration, add neat handwritten-style typography reading
exactly:

```
"coffee · mist · mountain"
"a quiet moment"

2024
```

Typography should be small, understated, dark blue-gray ink, with authentic
hand-lettered imperfections.

## Constraints

- Keep the Starbucks branding and sign clearly recognizable.
- Preserve Mount Fuji's distinctive shape.
- Do not add people, cars, extra buildings, or unnecessary objects.
- No photorealism, no digital 3D look, no heavy outlines, no oversaturated
  colors.

The final result should feel like a quiet, sophisticated Japanese watercolor
travel print made by hand.
